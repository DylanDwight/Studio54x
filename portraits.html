Portraits

Everything from professional solo head shots to natural, candid group photos — captured to remember or to gift.

Through an in-studio session, we capture the moments you want to keep. We provide framed prints, wall canvases, mini photo books, print boxes, coffee table albums, and more — our full catalog is available in the studio during your session.

Unsure how many prints you want? Because the session and the products are priced separately, you're free to purchase products after the session.

To inquire about availability, special requests, or a detailed estimate, please request a consultation.

The Carter-Jensen family

  • A digital copy of your choice of eight edited proofs
  • An 18"x24" framed portrait of the proof of your choice
  • 12 6"x4" prints of up to two proofs of your choice
  • 24 2"x3" prints of up to two proofs of your choice
