About us

A one-woman business that grew into a studio, a team, and a decade of Finger Lakes weddings, portraits, and events.

The studio

Founded in 2012

Studio 54x began as a private, one-woman business and has since expanded into a physical location with set backgrounds and props. The studio now employs several photographers and editors to ensure that only high-quality work leaves our doors.

The founder

Sarah Kim

Sarah has been taking photos since 1996 and began her professional career in 2003. She moved to Ithaca, NY in 2007 and has been serving the Finger Lakes area ever since.

As a longtime Ithacan, Sarah knows the area like the back of her hand — scouting outdoor locations and building a list of the most unique, beautiful backgrounds in the region.
