Events

As part of our on-location services we photograph events of any size — engagement parties, fundraiser galas, corporate dinners, and more.

Pricing is based on the number of guests, the subject of the photos, and the duration of the event. Because these factors vary so much, we recommend booking a consultation for an accurate estimate.
